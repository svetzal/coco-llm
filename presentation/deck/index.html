FIRST CODE IN THE TALK. It is training while this is on screen. The 6809 has a MUL instruction and it multiplies two UNSIGNED bytes. The model needs signed multiplication. The first version did it a bit at a time: correct, easy to explain, and 38.6 million instructions for the run. This is what replaced it. Now you play it

EXP-013 · the game opponent that learns

NEVER CUT. Someone from the room calls throws. It does not know the rules: RULES 0/25 says so. It does not know the player either: MEMORY n/rounds says how little it has. Both stay on screen. Six or eight rounds. It states its expectation BEFORE each throw. Around twenty-five rounds it has the rules; it never finishes learning the person. Press R in front of them and let it fall over. QUOTE THE HUMAN NUMBER: 80% against six synthetic players with habits. 52.8% against a recorded 200-round session with a person who was trying. Better than chance at three sigma, nowhere near enough to win. Five of six synthetic players had a habit and one did not. A person plays like the one that did not.
