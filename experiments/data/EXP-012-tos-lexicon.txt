# EXP-012 entity lexicon. Hand-tagged; this file is a design decision, not a
# measurement. See EXP-012-episode-titles.md.
#
# The phrases are what tools/measure_tos_tokenizations.py lifts out of the 79
# titles when the frame words are removed. The tag is what the phrase can
# grammatically follow, which is the whole reason generated titles read as
# English rather than as word salad:
#
#   NS   count noun, singular    THE CAGE / A CAGE / OF THE CAGE
#   NM   mass or abstract noun   THE TRUTH / OF TRUTH        (never "A TRUTH")
#   NPL  plural noun             THE TRIBBLES / OF TRIBBLES  (never "A ...")
#   PN   proper or possessive    GOTHOS / OF GOTHOS          (never "THE ...")
#
# Twelve phrases are dropped rather than tagged, because they are verb or
# adjective fragments left over by slotting a frame that is not nominal
# ("...GODS DESTROY", "...SHALL LEAD", "...IS HOLLOW"), or carry punctuation
# that only makes sense in the one title it came from ("MIRROR, MIRROR",
# "OPERATION - ANNIHILATE!", "ASSIGNMENT: EARTH"). They are listed at the
# bottom so the count reconciles: 101 kept + 12 dropped = 113 extracted.
